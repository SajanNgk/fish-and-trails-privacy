Fish & Trails

Draw the flow. Guide the fish. Explore the reef.
Privacy Policy · Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what Fish & Trails (the "game", Android package com.fishtrail.fish_trail) collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by an independent developer (GitHub: SajanNgk).

1. The short version

  • The game works offline. Your progress is saved on your device only.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no online leaderboard.
  • We do not ask for your name, address, phone number, location or payment details.
  • The game shows rewarded ads: ads you choose to watch in exchange for a reward. In regions that require it, you are asked for consent first.
  • We do not sell your data.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels completed, stars, score, coins, fish gear you own and wear) and your settings (music, sound effects, vibration, controls) are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this information. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your Android settings, removes it.

If you choose to import a level pack, the game reads the file you pick and stores the levels on your device. The file is not uploaded anywhere.

3. What we do not collect

The game itself does not use analytics, crash-reporting services, user accounts, or an online leaderboard, and does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. The only data that leaves your device is what the ad service described below handles.

4. Ads

The game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads (for example, to double the coins you earned, to keep playing after a loss, or to get free coins). Ads are always optional. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and device and ad-interaction data to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, under Google's own policies: policies.google.com/privacy. For this reason the game declares the Android advertising ID permission.

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states), the game shows a consent message through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from the "Ad privacy choices" button in the game's Settings, where available. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android settings.

5. Service providers

  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform: support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

If we add other services in future (for example analytics or purchases), we will update this policy first.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us and we will help get it removed.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep no personal data about you on our own servers, because the game has none. Your saved progress can be deleted by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage. Data that Google AdMob handles is retained under Google's own policies; to ask about it, use Google's tools linked above or contact us.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact us using the details below.

9. Security

Ads are loaded over encrypted connections by Google's ad software. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be reflected here.
